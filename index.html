    TURNING ACADEMIC CHAOS INTO CLARITY, ONE DEADLINE AT A TIME

    College students frequently juggle overlapping programming labs, research papers, exams, and team projects across fragmented apps and notebooks—often resulting in forgotten deadlines, time conflicts, and unnecessary academic stress.

    SyncDate solves this problem by bringing all tasks into a single intelligent workspace. Through continuous priority scoring, proactive deadline alerts, and automatic task ranking, SyncDate eliminates schedule clutter and tells students exactly what to focus on first, ensuring every deadline is met with confidence.

    How it works

    From to-do to done in four steps.

    1. Add a taskEnter what it is, where it happens, the task type and the due date and time.
    2. Rate itUse the sliders to set how urgent and how important it is. SyncDate classifies its priority for you.
    3. Follow the rankingYour schedule is sorted automatically, and reminders pop up in the bell above as deadlines get close.
    4. Mark it completeCheck it off and it moves to the Completed Tasks page while your progress bar fills up.
